Our Story

Books, Brews
& Big Hearts

Snoofé started as a tiny corner shelf and a coffee pot shared between friends. Today, we're a cozy little haven where the smell of fresh pages meets the smell of fresh espresso — built for readers, dreamers, and anyone who just needs a good cup and a good book.

How it began

A Little Shop With a Big Dream

It all started in 2021 with one shelf of secondhand novels and a secondhand espresso machine. What began as a weekend hobby quickly turned into a neighborhood favorite, where strangers became regulars and regulars became family.

What drives us

Crafted With Care, Served With Warmth

Every bean we brew and every book we shelve is chosen with the same question in mind — will this make someone's day a little softer? That's the whole point of Snoofé: a slower, warmer corner of the day.
